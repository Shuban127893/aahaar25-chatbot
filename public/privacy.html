Privacy Notice

How AAHAAR25 handles your information

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

When you order a lunch box or message us on WhatsApp, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your chosen delivery day and stop
  • Basic order details (what you ordered, order status, payment status)

We don't ask for or store anything beyond what's needed to get your order to you.

How we use it

We use your information only to:

  • Take and prepare your order
  • Coordinate delivery with our drivers
  • Send you order and delivery updates over WhatsApp
  • Answer questions you send us through WhatsApp chat

Who we share it with

We use a small number of outside services to run our ordering system, and your information passes through them only as needed to do that:

  • WhatsApp / Meta — the messaging platform your order conversation happens on
  • Square — to process your payment securely

We do not sell your information to anyone, and we don't share it with any other third party for marketing or advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for our own business and food-safety purposes. If you'd like your information removed from our systems, just reach out using the contact info below and we'll take care of it.

Keeping it safe

Our ordering system uses standard security practices to protect your information, including encrypted connections and restricted access to customer data.

Cancellations & refunds

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before the driver has left for delivery. Once a driver has been dispatched, refunds are not automatic - please call us directly to discuss options.

To cancel before dispatch, message "cancel" to us on WhatsApp, or call us at any time.

Questions?

If you have any questions about your information or this notice, call us at 704-234-8400 or message us on WhatsApp.
